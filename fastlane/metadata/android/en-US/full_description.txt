Frequency is a native Android peer-to-peer messaging app.

Communicate with nearby peers over Bluetooth LE mesh or discover peers and send
packets directly over a multicast-capable local Wi-Fi network. Bluetooth LE is
retained as a fallback when LAN delivery is unavailable.

Frequency is under active development and has not received an independent
security review. Direct peer messaging over mobile data is not yet implemented.
Local-network discovery may not work on Wi-Fi networks that block multicast or
isolate clients.
